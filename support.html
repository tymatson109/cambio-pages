Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Profile -> Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and match history right away.

How do I block or report another player?
From your Match history screen, tap a past opponent's name to block or report them. Blocked players will never be matched with you again.

I found a bug or the app crashed. What do I do?
Email tymatson109@gmail.com with a short description of what happened, what device you're using, and (if you can) a screenshot. That's the fastest way to get it fixed.
