Cambio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Cambio is a multiplayer card game app. This policy explains what information Cambio collects, how it's used, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

  • Email address - used to create and sign in to your account, either directly or through Sign in with Apple / Sign in with Google.
  • Username - chosen by you when you create your account. Your username is visible to other players you're matched or friended with.
  • Friend connections - the other Cambio players you've added as friends.
  • Gameplay data - match history, including who you played against, final scores, and match outcomes.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, or advertising identifiers. Cambio does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

How we use this information

Your information is used solely to operate the app: authenticating you, matching you with opponents, displaying your friends list, and showing your match history. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share information with

Cambio's backend is hosted on Supabase, which stores your account and gameplay data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider. If you sign in with Apple or Google, those companies process your authentication according to their own privacy policies. We do not share your information with any other third party.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: go to Profile -> Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your email, username, friend connections, and match history. This action cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

Cambio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
