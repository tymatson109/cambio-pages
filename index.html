How it plays

Four rules. That's the whole game.

Cambio is quick to learn and nearly impossible to master once your friends start bluffing about what they remember.

01

Peek, then forget nothing

At the start of the round you get one secret look at a couple of your own cards. Everything else is memory from there.

02

Royals have powers

Draw a 9 through King and you might get a peek at your own hand, a look at an opponent's, or a blind swap between two cards.

03

Slam, anytime

Spot a match on the discard pile? Slam it - on anyone's turn, not just yours. Guess wrong and you're giving a card away.

04

Call Cambio

Think your hand's low enough? Call it. Everyone else gets one last turn, then the lowest total wins the round.
